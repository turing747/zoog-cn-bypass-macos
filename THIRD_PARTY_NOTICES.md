The files `data/cn-ipv4.txt` and `data/cn-ipv6.txt` are generated from [APNIC allocation and assignment reports](https://ftp.apnic.net/stats/apnic/delegated-apnic-latest).

APNIC makes these reports freely available for download and use on the condition that APNIC is not held responsible for loss or damage arising from their use. APNIC does not guarantee accuracy, and the reports describe initial allocation or assignment rather than authoritative current geolocation. See the [APNIC statistics README](https://ftp.apnic.net/apnic/stats/apnic/README.TXT).
